## Animation

`render-art-film.py` renders a 28-second, 30 fps H.264 MP4. It uses a radial outline reveal, camera moves, a procedural rotating gyroscope, floating particles and a diagonal light sweep. `render-film.py` renders the separate 24-second photograph film. Source artwork is in `assets/films/art/`; final videos are in `assets/films/`. Both renderers require Pillow, NumPy and imageio-ffmpeg, and currently use the system Georgia and Arial fonts on macOS.
