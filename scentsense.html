ScentSense

What happens when a machine’s guess becomes something you can smell?

The device

My partner and I built a device that routes a facial-expression prediction to one of four scent channels. I wrote the software, connected the camera and scent channels to a Raspberry Pi, and worked through eight iterations of the housing.

A steadier prediction

The scent initially changed with each new frame. I added a vote across five frames before switching. That made the output steadier during the demonstration, where one student told me they were tired after the system read their expression as sad.

Room for a correction

A future interface could give the visitor a way to correct the label or choose the scent directly. The demonstration gave me a concrete reason to put that choice in the visitor’s hands.
