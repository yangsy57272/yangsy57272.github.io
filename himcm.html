Mathematical modeling / Team research

HiMCM

Two models for decisions under constraints, from computing footprints to building sweeps.

2024 · The footprint of computing

As team captain, I worked with my teammates to follow high-performance computing through manufacturing, transport, use and disposal. We modeled carbon emissions and water consumption, then explored how hardware replacement and electricity sources could change the footprint by 2030.

2025 · A building sweep under changing visibility

Our four-person team modeled how responders search rooms as smoke spreads and visibility changes. I helped the team align on a floor plan and time step before we combined our work. Within the simulation, a sweep that prioritized the most dangerous floor could take longer overall. The team earned a finalist designation.

What the assumptions control

The model held smoke behavior fixed when a door opened. Before using the model to recommend a route, I would test how changing that assumption affects who is reached first and how long a sweep takes.
