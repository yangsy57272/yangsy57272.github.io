01 / The red paper flower

The same question. Three times.

A fifth-grade student asked me the same grammar question three times in one class. Later, when she visited Shanghai from Henan, she gave me a small red flower and thanked me for being patient.

I used to count the lessons I had finished. She remembered whether she could ask again. The flower is easy to miss among the brighter stickers on my laptop. I keep it there.

Questions I kept working on

Computer Vision

Facial Expression & Identity Recognition System (Dlib)

I wanted to connect facial expressions with scent. At our school showcase, the device kept misreading my friends. Calling it “inaccurate” was easy; finding where it went wrong took longer. I worked through detection, alignment, features, and classification, noticing a shifted bounding box and too few “angry” examples. The project became a lesson in asking more specific questions.

Python Dlib Computer Vision CTB Competition (2026) Generation AI Hackathon
Mathematical Modeling

Emergency Evacuation Strategy Optimization (HIMCM 2024 & 2025)

How do you turn a building, its exits, and the people inside it into a useful model? Across two years of HIMCM, I explored evacuation routes under fire and gas-leak conditions. I worked with dynamic risk, visibility, and mortality models, translating assumptions into simulations and diagrams. The mathematics interested me most where it met a practical decision: which way should someone go?

Multi-agent Modeling Gompertz Functions HPC Simulation HIMCM Finalist Top 4%
Energy Engineering

TENG-Based Hybrid Wave Energy Harvesting Device (Conrad Challenge)

I worked on a buoy that collects wave energy, moving between SolidWorks, printed parts, laser-cut models, and the electronics. During a presentation, a judge recognized the technology and invited others to listen. I remember that moment of being heard while the idea was still taking shape. The prototype combines triboelectric and piezoelectric effects to turn motion into electricity.

TENG SolidWorks 3D Printing Energy Harvesting Conrad Silver Award

Four problems, AIME-style.

Every answer is an integer from 0 to 999. No calculator — that's the fun of it.

i.

How many positive integers less than 1000 are divisible by 7 but not by 11?

Count the multiples of 7, then subtract the multiples of 77.
ii.

In how many ways can 8 identical debate trophies be arranged on 3 different shelves (a shelf may be empty)?

Stars and bars: 8 stars, 2 bars.
iii.

How many positive integers less than 1000 are multiples of 3 or 5?

Inclusion–exclusion: count multiples of 3, add multiples of 5, subtract multiples of 15.
iv.

Find the remainder when 22026 is divided by 1000.

Work mod 8 and mod 125 separately. Powers of 2 mod 125 repeat every 100.
