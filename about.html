About

Hi, I’m Wendy.

I’m Shiyi Yang, a student in Shanghai. I build things, write songs, debate, and carry a camera.

Leaving the screen open

I used to sit at the back of the classroom so nobody could read an idea before I was ready to share it. A conversation with my dad about a poem by Du Fu helped. He heard my reading and offered his own. I could take his seriously and still keep mine.

“Being heard does not mean being overwritten.”

The laptop stickers

There are more than thirty stickers on my laptop. Some are peeling. I keep them anyway: a small red flower, a Citrus Circuits sticker, a reminder to leave the gap in a proof showing.

The red flower came from a student who asked the same grammar question three times. I used to count the lessons I had finished. She remembered whether she could ask again.
